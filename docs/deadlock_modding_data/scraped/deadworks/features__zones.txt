Zones & beams
Namespace:
DeadworksManaged.Api.Utils
for zones,
DeadworksManaged.Api
for beams
A
zone
is an invisible box in the world that reports when a player walks into it or out of it. Use one for a finish line, a checkpoint or a safe area.
A
beam
is a glowing line in the world that every player can see. Use beams to show players where a zone is.
