CCitadelPlayerController.RemoveWithPawns()
​
Removes this player from the game completely: their hero, any other pawn they control (such as a spectator's observer pawn), and this controller, together in the same tick.
By default a player who leaves keeps their controller and hero, so they can reconnect to them, and their portrait stays in the top bar. Call this from
IDeadworksPlugin.OnClientDisconnect
to drop them instead. Don't remove the hero or the controller on its own: either one left behind crashes clients or the server. To remove a player who is still connected,
CBasePlayerController.Kick
them.
