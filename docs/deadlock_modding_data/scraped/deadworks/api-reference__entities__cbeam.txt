Notes
​
Beams glow like a laser: they brighten whatever is behind them, so they always look a little see-through, and the world's lighting shades them. They can't be made solid or fullbright. Black beams are invisible, and lowering the alpha makes a beam fainter. Change the color at any time with
CBaseEntity.RenderColor
.
A beam stays until you remove it (
CBaseEntity.Remove
, or
RemoveAll
for a list) or the map changes.
