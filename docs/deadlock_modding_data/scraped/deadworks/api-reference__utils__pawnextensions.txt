PawnExtensions.Freeze()
​
Holds the hero in place. Until the freeze ends the player can only look around: they can't move, jump, dash, shoot or use abilities. With
invulnerable
, which is the default, weapons and abilities can't hurt them either.

PawnExtensions.Kill()
​
Kills the hero. The damage skips resistances and protection, so it works even on an invulnerable hero, and the game treats it as an ordinary death: they wait out the usual death timer unless you pass
respawnImmediately
