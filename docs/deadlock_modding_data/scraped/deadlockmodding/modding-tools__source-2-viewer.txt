# Source 2 Viewer
URL: https://deadlockmodding.pages.dev/modding-tools/source-2-viewer

Source 2 Viewer
is a powerful tool that allows you to browse VPK archives, view, extract, and decompile Source 2 assets, including maps, models, materials, textures, sounds, and more.
Official Website
GitHub
Decompilation
Right click on your asset of choice, then select Decompile & Export to decompile it into your content directory. Usually it’s recommended to match the filepath of the original asset, for example if it was
materials/cobble/cobble_
floor_
01_
blend.vmat
, then it should be the same if you’re replacing it.
To decompile multiple assets, right click on a folder or select multiple with Ctrl+ Left Click then right click, then select Decompile and Export. This time it’s recommended to put your asset into the root folder of your addon like this:
content/citadel_
addons/myaddon
due to Source 2 Viewer extracting multiple assets in their respective directories automatically.
